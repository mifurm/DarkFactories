## 8. Expansion levers E1–E5 (optional; OFF in Base and Conservative, ON in Upside)

Each lever has its own Azure consumption (priced from the Retail Prices API), its own customer-side costs and its own risk-adjusted benefit. The **expansion case** below applies the Upside adoption of all five levers on top of the Base inputs (E1 50%, E2 75%, E3 50%, E4 100%, E5 100%).

### 8.1 Economics per deploying plant (steady state, risk-adjusted, USD thousand per year unless stated)

| Lever | Azure run cost | Other run cost | Benefit | Net | One-time cost | Simple payback | Enterprise-fixed items (at 8 plants) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| E1 Machine-vision fleet | 29 | 89 | 493 | 375 | 442 | 14 months | — |
| E2 High-frequency condition monitoring | 15 | 44 | 341 | 282 | 282 | 12 months | — |
| E3 Continuous sim-first what-if at scale | 47 | 38 | 371 | 287 | 132 | 6 months | — |
| E4 Enterprise data-platform modernisation | 5 | 30 | 240 | 205 | 121 | 7 months | Azure $298,260, other run $50,400, one-time $495,000 |
| E5 OT security & connectivity hardening | 26 | 41 | 52 | -15 | 122 | none | Azure $15,330, other run $0, one-time $0 |

* **E1 Machine-vision fleet** — Edge inference stays customer-side, so Azure carries the image archive, retraining and low-confidence escalations: modest per plant, material across a fleet. Pays back through inspection labour and escape cost.
* **E2 High-frequency condition monitoring** — Small for Azure: the edge extracts features and only scheduled raw snapshots travel, so storage and compute are a few thousand dollars per plant (continuous raw streaming multiplies it). Its value is earlier failure detection.
* **E3 Continuous sim-first what-if at scale** — Discrete-event simulation is cheap; Azure consumption becomes material only with Monte-Carlo rollouts at scale or physics-level fidelity. Its value is earning higher autonomy levels.
* **E4 Enterprise data-platform modernisation** — The largest single step: Fabric capacity moves from plant level to enterprise scale. Power BI Pro and other Microsoft 365 licences are not Azure consumption.
* **E5 OT security & connectivity hardening** — A risk and compliance lever: it adds Sentinel, Firewall Premium, a second ExpressRoute path and Defender for Servers, but does not pay for itself on expected-loss value alone. Defender for IoT OT licences are Microsoft 365, not ACR.
