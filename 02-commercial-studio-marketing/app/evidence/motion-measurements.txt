# Cinematic motion (CS-25): how much do the generated clips really move?
# Measure: dense Farneback optical flow between frames 10 apart (0.42 s at 24 fps) on 256x144 gray frames, 8 pairs per clip; "non_rigid" = mean flow magnitude (px) that is
# LEFT OVER after the best global affine fit of the flow field, i.e. what a camera move on a still (zoom, pan, roll) cannot explain. Code: app/src/spotlight/motion/quality.py,
# CLI: scripts/measure_motion.py. Levels: low < 0.32 <= subtle < 1.0 <= rich.
# A plain frame-difference check cannot do this job: a still with a Ken-Burns zoom changes every pixel too (the smoke test's "footage not frozen" check passes for it).
