# Polish strategy: an English prompt heading leaked into an insight's source label (found by the live Polish journeys, fixed, re-verified live)

## Finding (journeys_pl.py, full run on revision ca-studio-web--0000008, 19 Nov 2026; app/evidence/motion-e2e-live-pl.txt: 35/36)
FAIL  strategy agent (Polish territories, SSE with ?lang=)   strategy: english: '[Learnings from past performance]' — word: from [SPAN text-[12px] text-espresso-400]
Cause: the strategy prompt of a Polish campaign carried English section headings ("Citations from the Brand Brain:", "Learnings from past performance:"); the model copied one of them
into the free-text `source` of an insight. Not related to CS-25; present since the Polish release, appears only when the model copies the heading (36/36 passed on the earlier run).

## Fix (commit da99d6bf)
cs05_strategy._summary_for_prompt(ctx, lang): Polish headings for a Polish campaign ("Cytaty z Brand Brain:", "Wnioski z dotychczasowych wyników:", "(pewność …)", "(brak)"); the English prompt is byte-identical.
Test: tests/test_pl_pipeline.py::test_the_strategy_prompt_has_polish_section_headings_for_polish_and_unchanged_english_ones. Backend: 1,432 passed + 8 xfail.
